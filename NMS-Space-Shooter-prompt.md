# No Man's Sky-inspired 3D Third-Person Shooter (MVP+++)

Create an MVP+++ of a 3D third-person shooter inspired by No Man's Sky, built in HTML, with PvE, loot, and grinding mechanics. Use realistic 3D models of stars and spaceships—no simplified, blocky models!

Include multiple ship classes and subclasses, a wide variety of weapons, and extensive customization. Add shops on planets where players can land and trade resources mined or obtained from ships. Enemies should have varying levels.

There must be a map/radar with zoom functionality and access to a galaxy map. Everything should have a sci-fi space aesthetic. Include login functionality and save progress in the cache.

Pack in as much as you possibly can. Good luck
